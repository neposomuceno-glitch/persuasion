Truth Detective Guide

Be smart online! Learn to spot fake news, block sneaky tricks, and take care of your mind.

Easy-Read Chart · Grades 4+
🔍

Spot Fake News

Some stories are made up. Here is how to check.

  • 1
    Check the source.Who posted this? Is it a real news group, or just a random account?
  • 2
    Look for other reports.Do other trusted places say the same thing?
  • 3
    Check the date.Old news can trick you. Make sure the story is new.
  • 4
    Notice big feelings.If a post makes you mad or scared right away, stop and think first.
  • 5
    Check the picture.Photos can be changed. Ask an adult to help you look closer.
🛑

Block Sneaky Tricks

Ads and posts can try to trick you. Watch for these tricks.

  • 1
    Ask what they want.Ads and posts often want your money, your click, or your vote.
  • 2
    Watch for name-calling.If someone attacks a person instead of explaining ideas, be careful.
  • 3
    Slow down before you share.Wait one minute. Do not share right away.
  • 4
    Spot trick words."Everyone knows this" or "they don't want you to see this" are warning signs.
  • 5
    Look at both sides.Good information shows more than one side of a story.
💜

Take Care of Your Mind

Apps are built to keep you scrolling. Protect yourself.

  • 1
    Take breaks.Put the phone down. Rest your eyes and your brain.
  • 2
    Don't chase likes.Likes do not measure how good or important you are.
  • 3
    Notice your feelings.If an app makes you feel sad or jealous, take a break from it.
  • 4
    Turn off some alerts.Buzzes and pings pull you back in. Turn some off.
  • 5
    Talk to a trusted adult.If something online feels wrong or scary, tell a parent, teacher, or trusted adult.
